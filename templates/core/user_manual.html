GuardLink User Manual

Guide for clients and administrators

1. Client account

Create an account using Create account. Sign in with your email and password, complete the 6-digit email verification code, then submit guard requests from the client dashboard.

2. Submit a request

Select the rank, shift, guard count, coverage dates, and site details. The administrator will approve or reject the request.

3. Administrator workflow

From the admin dashboard, approve requests, assign guards, record attendance, calculate payroll, create invoices, and issue or mark invoices paid.

4. Password recovery

Choose Forgot password on the login page and follow the emailed reset link.

5. Reports and audit trail

Administrators can filter and print invoice/payment reports, export CSV, and review all recorded sensitive actions under the audit trail.

6. Troubleshooting

If a verification or reset email is not received, check spam and confirm the account email address. Do not share passwords or verification codes.
